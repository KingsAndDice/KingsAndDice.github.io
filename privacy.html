Kings & Dice — Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Kings & Dice is a free browser game (dice chess) operated by Inaam-Ullah Rana ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and what choices you have. Contact: inaamr1996@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we store your chosen in-game username and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in readable form). You may optionally add an email address for password recovery; recovery codes are sent to that address.
  • Google sign-in (optional). If you sign in with Google, Google shares your Google account ID and email address with us. We store these only to operate your game account and to let you sign in again. Your Google identity is never shown to other players — only your chosen in-game name is public.
  • Gameplay data. Match results, ratings, rankings, tournament records, virtual coin balances for account holders, and records of public matches (including replays that other players may watch). Coins are purely virtual, have no cash value, and cannot be bought or cashed out.
  • Chat. Messages you send in game rooms and in the global chat are delivered to other players. The global chat keeps only the most recent messages in memory and they are cleared when the server restarts. Reports you file about other players are stored so the game owner can review them.
  • On your device. The game saves settings, display name, guest coin balance and cosmetic choices in your browser's local storage. These stay on your device.
  • Hosting logs. The game is hosted on GitHub Pages and the game server runs on Render; those providers may process standard technical data such as IP addresses in their server logs under their own privacy policies.

What we do not collect

  • No payment information — the game is free and sells nothing.
  • No advertising trackers or analytics cookies set by the game itself.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

How we use information

Only to operate the game: signing you in, running matches and rankings, preventing cheating and abuse, reviewing player reports, and recovering accounts. Other players can see your in-game username, your public profile (ratings, divisions, tournament record) and your public match replays — never your email address, password, or Google identity.

Your choices

  • You can play as a guest without creating an account.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and its associated personal data at any time by emailing inaamr1996@gmail.com. Rated match records that other players' ratings depend on may be retained in anonymized form.

Children

Kings & Dice is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
